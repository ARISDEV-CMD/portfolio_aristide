## Brand & Style

### Vision & Personality
The design system positions a Senior Full Stack & DevOps Engineer as an institutional, authoritative, and high-impact technologist. The aesthetic synthesizes the architectural discipline of DevOps infrastructure with the crisp, modern execution of developer-first tech companies (Linear, Vercel, Stripe). It conveys competence, stability, precision, and executive craftsmanship.

### Target Audience
- Tech leads, CTOs, and Engineering Directors evaluating full-stack competence, cloud governance, and CI/CD pipelines.
- Enterprise recruiters seeking high-reliability architects.
- Collaborative engineering peers expecting clean interfaces, structural documentation, and production-grade fidelity.

### Design Movement
**Modern Corporate Developer Minimal** with subtle architectural luxury:
- Monolithic, dark slate structures balanced with surgical micro-lines and precision typography.
- Deep Navy surfaces establish an unyielding foundation.
- Kinetic Orange drives interactive clarity and execution cues.
- Subtle hairline Gold trims supply institutional pedigree without decorative excess.
- Strict WCAG AA contrast ensures crisp readability across architectural schematics, terminal blocks, and metrics cards.

## Colors

### Strict Palette Governance
The visual architecture enforces an uncompromising 4-color foundation:
1. **Deep Navy (`#0D1B3E`)**: Structural canvas, application shells, headers, footers, code container backgrounds, and section backdrops (60% visual balance).
2. **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Primary typography on navy, secondary badge fills, high-contrast light card backgrounds, and crisp icon strokes (25% visual balance).
3. **Electric Orange (`#FF8A00`)**: Interactive focus, primary action buttons, hover states, deployment indicators, and live metrics (10% visual balance).
4. **Institutional Gold (`#D4AF37`)**: Sub-millimeter borders, structural dividers, certificate badges, and tier-1 achievement frames (5% visual balance).

### Accessible Functional Derivations
- **Text on Deep Navy**: `#FFFFFF` for headings and titles (contrast 14.8:1, WCAG AAA). `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` for secondary body (contrast 8.6:1, WCAG AAA).
- **Text on Orange**: `#0D1B3E` (contrast 6.2:1, WCAG AA Large & Normal).
- **Borders & Partitions**: Hairlines use `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)` or `#1E3368` to keep structural dividers discreet and free of clutter.

## Typography

### Structural Approach
The type hierarchy relies on **Geist** for all display, heading, and body layers. Its mechanical geometry, tightened tracking, and neutral letterforms deliver Vercel-like surgical precision. For technical metadata, commit hashes, CI/CD telemetry, and infrastructure specs, **JetBrains Mono** supplies strict tabular alignment and legible code clarity.

### Usage Standards
- **Display & Headings**: Negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) maintains visual density and impact, even at large scales.
- **Body Text**: Rendered with comfortable line-height ratios (`1.55` to `1.6`) to sustain effortless scanning of architectural case studies and deployment post-mortems.
- **Labels & System Chips**: JetBrains Mono at 11–13px in uppercase or syntax notation (`v2.4.0`, `k8s:cluster-01`, `POST /api/v1`).

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column symmetrical fluid grid bounded inside a max-width container of `1280px`. Gutters set to `1.5rem` (`24px`), margins to `3rem` (`48px`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px)**: 8-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column responsive grid with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins.

### Spatial Rhythm
Spacers enforce an 8px base rhythm (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.5rem`, `4rem`, `6rem`). 
- Large vertical spans (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) isolate major portfolio chapters (e.g., Infrastructure, Projects, Certifications).
- Compact spans (`space-sm` and `space-md`) establish visual groupings between status badges, metric counters, and associated headings.

## Elevation & Depth

### Principles
In keeping with high-grade engineering portals, visual depth avoids heavy, artificial drop-shadows. Instead, depth is articulated through **tonal layering**, **hairline borders**, and **contained directional glows**.

### Hierarchy of Surfaces
1. **Ground Zero (Canvas)**: `#0D1B3E` (Pure matte navy foundation).
2. **Elevated Surface (Cards & Sidebars)**: `#14244F` paired with an outline of `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.16)` or `1px solid #1E3368`.
3. **Interactive Hover**: Cards transition background color to `#182C61` with the gold border opacity moving to `0.45` and an ambient glow of `0 8px 30px rgba(13, 27, 62, 0.6)`.
4. **Primary CTA Elevation**: The orange interactive elements carry an optical halo: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(255, 138, 0, 0.28)`.
5. **Overlays & Modals**: `#0D1B3E` at `95%` opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a continuous perimeter border in `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

### Form Geometry
The shape language uses `roundedness: 1` (`0.25rem` / `4px` base radius), delivering crisp, industrial edges that mirror modern CLI and cloud orchestration consoles:
- **Buttons, Text Inputs, and Selectors**: `4px` corner radius.
- **System Badges & Status Chips**: `4px` corner radius for technical badges, or fully rounded `pill` (`9999px`) exclusively for live pulse chips (`● Available`).
- **Cards, Code Sandboxes & Panels**: `8px` corner radius (`rounded-lg`), sustaining a compact boundary with tight outer clipping.
- **Dividers & Strokes**: Precise 1px hairline construction. Heavy bevels and thick radii are strictly avoided.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Deploy / Contact / Download CV)**: Background `#FF8A00`, text `#0D1B3E`, font Geist 600, radius `4px`. Subtle `0 2px 12px rgba(255, 138, 0, 0.3)` aura. Hover elevates lightness to `#FF9D26`. Focus state exhibits a `2px solid #FFFFFF` offset ring.
- **Secondary Action (Architecture Review / Case Study)**: Background transparent, border `1px solid #D4AF37`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover triggers `background: rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Text `#FFFFFF`, transparent surface, hover reveals background `#14244F` with text `#FF8A00`.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- Background: Surface `#14244F`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`.
- Header: JetBrains Mono project classification badge paired with a pure white title.
- Metrics & Tech Stack: Arranged in horizontal chips with deep navy backgrounds and subtle gold borders.

### Status Chips & Metadata Badges
- **Active / Operational**: Background `rgba(255, 138, 0, 0.12)`, border `1px solid #FF8A00`, text `#FF8A00`. Accompanied by a pulsing 6px radial indicator.
- **Institutional / Certified**: Background `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)`, border `1px solid #D4AF37`, text `#D4AF37`.

### Terminal & Code Blocks
- Container: Background `#09122B`, inner border `1px solid #1E3368`.
- Header bar: Title in Geist 13px with subtle session status, copy button styled with `#FF8A00` active confirmation.
- Code Text: JetBrains Mono 13px, pure white text, `#FF8A00` for reserved commands, `#D4AF37` for parameters and flags.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs & Textareas**: Background `#09122B`, border `1px solid #1E3368`, text `#FFFFFF`, placeholder `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`. Focus switches border directly to `#FF8A00` with zero outline offset.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Size `16px`, border `1px solid #D4AF37`, checked background `#FF8A00` with `#0D1B3E` glyph.

### Navigation Header & Dock
- Fixed top anchor with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, background `rgba(13, 27, 62, 0.85)`.
- Bottom edge defined by an ultrathin `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)` rule.
- Links: Geist 14px 500 in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`, transitioning to `#FF8A00` when active or hovered.
